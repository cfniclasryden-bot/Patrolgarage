VK56 Valve Cover Cost

Part of
Nissan Patrol Y62 Dubai: The Complete Owner's Guide →
UPDATED · MAY 2026

Quick answer: Replacing both valve cover gaskets on a Y62 Nissan Patrol VK56VD 5.6L V8 in Dubai typically costs between AED 1,400 and AED 2,800 at a specialist workshop, covering OEM-grade gaskets for both cylinder banks plus three to five hours of labour. Dealer pricing runs higher. The wide spread comes down to whether the spark plug tube seals need replacing at the same time, which we almost always recommend on any Patrol past 80,000 km.

The VK56VD is a big engine and a good one, but it has a known tendency to seep oil from the valve covers once heat cycles and Dubai summers start doing their work. We see this regularly at Patrol Garage, especially on Y62 models from the 2012 to 2017 production run. The ambient temperature here sits at 45°C to 50°C in peak summer, and the radiant heat from Sheikh Zayed Road tarmac pushes past 70°C. That kind of sustained heat hardens rubber gaskets faster than temperate climates, and the VK56VD runs its covers close to the intake manifold with limited airflow around them. The result is a gasket that can start weeping as early as 70,000 km, particularly at the rear corners of each bank. Left alone, the oil runs down onto the exhaust manifold, burns off, and fills the cabin with that faint cooked-oil smell every time you stop. Eventually it finds the spark plug wells and you get misfires on P0300 through P0308. This guide gives you real 2026 cost numbers, explains what the job actually involves on the VK56VD, and helps you decide when to act.

What does a valve cover gasket do on the VK56VD?

The valve cover gasket seals the aluminium valve cover to the cylinder head, keeping pressurised oil inside the top end of the engine while the camshafts and valvetrain are running.

The VK56VD is a V8, so it has two cylinder banks and two valve covers, one per side. Each cover is sealed with a main perimeter gasket and a set of spark plug tube seals, typically six per bank on this engine. The tube seals sit around each spark plug bore and stop oil from pooling at the bottom of the plug wells. When those seals harden and crack, oil soaks the plug boots, the ignition coils start misfiring, and you end up paying for coils you would not otherwise need. This is why treating the tube seals as a separate optional item is a false economy on a high-mileage Patrol.

The VK56VD also has a variable valve timing system with oil control valves at each bank. If cover removal is already on the table, it is worth inspecting those OCV filters while access is good, since sludge in the filters produces the same P0010 or P0015 cam timing codes that owners sometimes chase expensively without finding the real cause.

What are the signs that your Y62 valve cover gaskets are failing?

The most obvious sign is oil sitting on top of the engine or running down the side of the block, often visible as a dark, slightly crusty residue along the lower edge of the valve cover.

Other things we see on cars that come in:

  • A burnt oil smell from the engine bay, strongest after the engine has been running at temperature and then switched off
  • Oil-soaked spark plug boots when the ignition coils are pulled out
  • Random misfire codes P0300 to P0308 with no other obvious cause
  • Oil level dropping slowly without any external puddle on the ground, because small seeps burn off before they can drip
  • Blue-grey exhaust smoke on startup that clears quickly
  • A ticking or tapping from the valve area that changes with oil pressure

If you are adding more than half a litre of oil between services and the car has no visible external leak, do not assume the engine is burning it. Get the valve covers inspected first.

How much does Y62 valve cover gasket replacement cost in Dubai in 2026?

For a full dual-bank replacement on the VK56VD at a specialist workshop in Dubai, expect to pay between AED 1,400 and AED 2,800 all in, parts and labour.

Here is how that breaks down in practice:

  • OEM or OEM-equivalent gasket set, both banks: AED 350 to AED 700 depending on source and brand
  • Spark plug tube seals, both banks (12 seals total): AED 150 to AED 300
  • Labour, three to five hours at specialist rates: AED 900 to AED 1,800

The labour range is wide because the intake manifold and several ancillary components need to move before the right-hand bank cover can come off cleanly. Some workshops quote the job without removing those components and instead work around them. That approach saves an hour on the bench but increases the risk of a stress crack on a plastic vacuum line or a disturbed connector that causes a different fault code two weeks later.

Authorised Nissan dealers in Dubai typically quote higher. Expect AED 3,000 to AED 4,500 at dealer labour rates, including a marked-up Nissan-branded gasket kit. [NEEDS_SOURCE: named dealer quote]

Ras Al Khor and Al Quoz workshops generally sit at the lower end of specialist pricing. Mobile mechanics exist for this job but we would not recommend them for the VK56VD specifically, because the torque sequence on the valve cover bolts is precise and the OCV filter access is easiest on a lift.

What factors push the cost up or down?

The base job is straightforward. Costs climb when other things are found or combined.

Factors that increase the bill:

  • A warped or cracked valve cover (plastic covers on some builds crack at the bolt bosses, adding AED 600 to AED 1,200 for a replacement cover)
  • Damaged ignition coils found during plug well inspection, typically AED 200 to AED 400 per coil on the VK56VD
  • OCV filter cleaning or replacement, which adds AED 200 to AED 500 per bank but is worth doing if the car has not had it done before
  • Combining the job with a spark plug replacement, which makes sense since the plugs are already out. Iridium plugs for the VK56VD run AED 80 to AED 120 each, so 8 plugs adds AED 640 to AED 960 in parts
  • Sludge inside the covers from infrequent oil changes, which adds cleaning time

Factors that keep costs down:

  • Catching the leak early before it contaminates the coils
  • Choosing aftermarket OEM-equivalent gaskets from a reputable supplier rather than dealer-branded parts
  • Combining with a scheduled major service so some labour overlaps

Why does Dubai's climate make this job more urgent than elsewhere?

Rubber compounds in gaskets degrade through repeated thermal cycling. Every time the engine warms up and cools down, the gasket expands and contracts. In a climate where summer ambient temperatures already sit at 45°C to 50°C before the engine adds its own heat, the starting point of each cycle is far higher than in Europe or North America. The gasket material spends more of its life near its upper temperature limit.

The UAE government has noted that vehicle maintenance intervals need adjustment for the local environment. Per u.ae, all vehicles registered in the UAE are subject to roadworthiness standards that include engine integrity checks, which means oil leaks are not just a performance issue but a compliance one when the car goes for its annual test.

Dust and sand from trips to Al Qudra or Liwa also matter. Fine particles stick to any external oil film and build up into an abrasive paste that accelerates wear on everything the oil touches outside the engine. A small seep in a dry climate is an annoyance. In the UAE, it collects grit and becomes a grinding compound against hoses, brackets, and wiring looms near the leak path.

Per the RTA, vehicles that fail inspection due to visible oil leaks require rectification before registration can be renewed. A valve cover seep that seems minor at 80,000 km can be the reason a car fails its Mulkiya renewal at 120,000 km.

Can you drive a Y62 with a leaking valve cover gasket?

You can, for a short time, but the risk grows the longer you leave it.

A small external seep that is not reaching the plug wells or the exhaust is a low-urgency fault. Top up the oil, monitor the level every two to three days, and book the repair within two to four weeks. Do not ignore a dropping oil level. The VK56VD holds approximately 6.5 litres of oil and can develop low oil pressure symptoms if it drops more than 1.5 litres without being topped up.

If the leak is reaching the spark plug tubes, the urgency is higher. Oil-contaminated plug boots break down the ignition insulation and coils fail prematurely. One coil is AED 200 to AED 400. Eight coils is a serious bill that a timely gasket replacement would have prevented.

If oil is dripping onto the exhaust manifold, book the car in immediately. The burning smell is the least of the problems. Oil on a hot exhaust is a fire risk, and in a vehicle used for off-road trips in remote areas like Liwa, that is not a theoretical concern.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the valve cover gasket replacement take on a Y62 VK56VD?

The job typically takes three to five hours on the VK56VD. The left-hand bank is more accessible and comes off faster. The right-hand bank requires moving intake components, which is where most of the time goes. If spark plug tube seals and OCV filters are included in the same visit, add another 30 to 60 minutes. A full-day booking is standard at most Ras Al Khor workshops for this job.

Should I replace both valve cover gaskets at the same time or just the leaking one?

Replace both at the same time. The labour to access the second bank when you are already halfway through the job is marginal, and both gaskets have been through the same heat cycles. If the right bank is leaking, the left bank is usually not far behind. Doing both in one visit costs significantly less than two separate jobs, and you avoid repeating the disassembly of intake components.

What gasket brand should I use for the Y62 VK56VD in Dubai?

Genuine Nissan gaskets are the safest choice for the VK56VD. Aftermarket sets from Fel-Pro or Victor Reinz are also acceptable if they include the tube seals and grommets in the kit. Avoid unbranded gasket sets that do not specify the rubber compound, as cheap materials harden and crack faster in UAE temperatures. For a vehicle used heavily off-road, OEM or premium aftermarket is the right call regardless of the small price difference.

Will a valve cover gasket leak cause my Y62 to fail its UAE vehicle inspection?

Yes, it can. The RTA's inspection process checks for active oil leaks, and a seeping valve cover that has left visible oil on engine components or the underside of the hood bay is a fail point. If your registration is coming up and you know the gaskets are leaking, fix them before the inspection rather than after. The repair cost is lower than dealing with a failed test, a retest fee, and the risk of driving on an expired registration.
