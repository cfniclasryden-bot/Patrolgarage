The Y62 Patrol is a capable machine, but no drivetrain component lasts forever when it is working through 45°C summers, coastal salt air, and weekend runs through Al Qudra or Big Red. CV joints and axle shafts are among the components we see come through our Ras Al Khor workshop regularly. The failure pattern is almost always the same: a rubber boot cracks from heat cycling, grease escapes, abrasive sand finds its way into the joint, and within a few thousand kilometres the clicking starts. At that point, a AED 100 boot replacement has turned into a AED 1,200 shaft job. Understanding what the repair involves, what it costs, and when you can get away with a cheaper fix is practical knowledge every Y62 owner in the UAE should have. Whether your Patrol is a daily on Sheikh Zayed Road or a weekend desert rig, the guidance below is specific to the Y62 platform and UAE conditions.

What is a CV joint and how does it work on the Y62?

A CV (constant velocity) joint transfers drive torque from the axle to the wheel at a constant rotational speed, regardless of the steering angle or suspension travel.

The Y62 Patrol uses an independent front suspension setup with a solid rear axle. That combination means the front axle shafts each carry two CV joints: an inner joint connecting to the differential and an outer joint at the wheel hub. The rear axle is a solid unit, so the rear does not use CV joints in the same way. When Y62 owners report clicking or vibration, the front outer CV joint is the most frequent culprit, especially on vehicles used for tight off-road manoeuvring where the joint works at full steering lock.

Each joint is sealed inside a rubber boot filled with grease. That boot is doing serious work in the UAE. Per UAE government guidance on vehicle safety standards, all vehicle mechanical components including drive axle assemblies must meet roadworthiness requirements at the time of registration renewal. A cracked boot with visible grease spray will fail an inspection. In Dubai, the RTA conducts periodic vehicle inspections through authorised centres, and a failed boot is a straightforward defect that gets flagged.

What are the symptoms of a failing CV joint on a Patrol Y62?

The clearest sign is a clicking or popping sound when turning, particularly at low speeds in a car park or making a tight turn on a desert track.

Beyond the click, here is what to watch for:

  • Clicking or clunking when turning at low speed (outer CV joint, typically the side closest to the wheel)
  • Vibration through the steering wheel or floor during acceleration, especially at highway speed on Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (inner CV joint wear)
  • Grease splattered on the inside of the wheel or on the lower control arm (torn boot, joint now running dry)
  • A shudder when pulling away from a standstill, particularly under load
  • Complete loss of drive to one front wheel in severe failure cases

If you see grease but hear no click yet, act immediately. The joint may still be serviceable with a boot replacement and fresh grease. Once the clicking starts, the joint itself is worn and needs replacement as a shaft assembly.

What causes Y62 CV joint failure faster in UAE conditions?

Heat is the primary driver. Ambient temperatures in Dubai reach 45°C to 50°C in summer, with tarmac surface temperatures exceeding 70°C. Rubber boots exposed to these temperatures over multiple years become brittle. Off-road use compounds the damage because at full steering lock the boot is already at maximum flex, and a boot that has stiffened from heat exposure will crack under that stress.

Sand ingress is the secondary issue. Once a boot cracks in the desert, fine silica sand enters the joint and acts as an abrasive. A joint that might run for another 30,000 km with a clean grease pack can be destroyed within weeks once contaminated with sand.

Coastal salt humidity, particularly for owners in areas like Jumeirah or along the coast toward Jebel Ali, accelerates metal corrosion on the joint splines. High-mileage Y62 units (above 150,000 km) that have never had a boot inspection are the most common cases we deal with. Aftermarket lift kits also change the operating angle of the front axle shafts, putting additional stress on the inner CV joint.

How much does CV joint replacement cost on a Y62 in the UAE?

For the Y62 specifically, expect to pay AED 800 to AED 1,500 per side for a complete front axle shaft replacement including labour at a specialist workshop. Here is how the costs break down:

  • CV boot replacement only (boot, grease, labour): AED 80 to AED 200 per boot. Only viable if the joint itself is not worn.
  • Aftermarket axle shaft replacement (quality brands such as GKN or equivalent): AED 500 to AED 900 per shaft including labour.
  • OEM Nissan axle shaft: AED 900 to AED 1,500 per shaft including labour at an independent specialist. Dealer pricing will sit above this range.
  • Both front shafts replaced together: AED 1,000 to AED 2,800 depending on parts choice. If one shaft is worn, the other is often not far behind on high-mileage vehicles, so doing both at the same visit saves labour costs.

Labour time for a front shaft on the Y62 is typically 1.5 to 3 hours per side. If the hub nut is corroded or the joint has seized, time can extend. Workshops in Al Quoz or Ras Al Khor that handle Y62 units regularly will have the correct hub nut socket and won't lose time sourcing tools.

Rear axle shaft work on the solid rear axle, if the shaft itself cracks or the flange fails, costs AED 500 to AED 1,500 per shaft at aftermarket pricing, consistent with pricing for other solid-axle 4WD vehicles in this segment.

OEM vs aftermarket: which shaft should you choose for a Y62?

For a Y62 used primarily on-road, a quality aftermarket shaft from a reputable supplier is a sensible choice. Brands supplying the GCC market that use OEM-spec materials and correct joint geometry are available at mid-range prices and carry a warranty. Avoid unbranded shafts sold purely on price: they frequently use softer steel that wears faster and boots that crack within 12 months in UAE temperatures.

For a Y62 used seriously off-road, particularly vehicles that run modified suspension or are regularly at Big Red or Liwa, OEM or heavy-duty aftermarket shafts are worth the extra cost. The Y62's VK56VD 5.6L V8 produces 400 hp and significant torque, and at full steering lock on a steep dune face the force through the outer CV joint is substantial. A budget shaft on that kind of use will not last.

If the vehicle is still within warranty or you are building a service history for resale, genuine Nissan parts keep the documentation clean.

Can you drive a Y62 with a failing CV joint in Dubai?

A cracked boot with no joint noise is drivable in the short term, but it should be booked in within a week. An actively clicking joint should not be ignored for more than a few days of light use. A fully failed joint can lock up or separate, which at highway speed on Sheikh Zayed Road is a serious safety situation.

Per the Roads and Transport Authority, vehicles in Dubai must be maintained in a safe and roadworthy condition. A vehicle with a failed CV joint that causes loss of steering control or drive would be considered mechanically defective under RTA roadworthiness standards. Beyond the safety and legal aspect, continuing to drive on a failed joint risks damaging the wheel bearing, hub, and brake components, adding AED 1,000 to AED 3,000 in further repairs.

How to get an accurate quote for Y62 CV joint work in Dubai

Workshop quotes for axle work vary significantly in the UAE. To compare quotes properly, be specific about what you need. Ask each workshop to quote separately for the boot replacement option and the full shaft replacement option. Ask whether the quote includes an OEM or aftermarket part and what brand they intend to fit. Ask whether the wheel alignment needs to be checked after the work (it often does on the front).

Alignment after front axle work on the Y62 typically adds AED 150 to AED 300 to the job. It is not always necessary if the suspension geometry was not disturbed, but it is worth checking on a vehicle used off-road.

A specialist workshop familiar with the Y62 platform will be able to diagnose by sound alone whether you are looking at a boot job or a full shaft. A quick visual inspection of the boot and a test drive with tight turns will confirm it. If a workshop wants to quote for a full shaft without checking the joint condition first, get a second opinion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a CV joint replacement cost for a Nissan Patrol Y62 in Dubai?

A full front axle shaft replacement on a Y62 in Dubai costs AED 800 to AED 1,500 per side including labour, depending on whether OEM or quality aftermarket parts are used. If the boot is caught early before joint wear, a boot replacement costs AED 80 to AED 200 per boot. Both front shafts replaced together at the same visit typically costs AED 1,000 to AED 2,800.

What does a clicking noise when turning mean on a Nissan Patrol Y62?

Clicking or popping when turning at low speed on a Y62 almost always points to a worn outer CV joint. The outer joint works at its maximum angle when the steering is on full lock, and a worn or dry joint produces a distinct click under those conditions. At this stage the joint needs replacement as a shaft assembly, not just a boot change. Delaying the repair risks further damage to the wheel hub and bearing.

How long does a CV joint last on a Nissan Patrol Y62 in the UAE?

Under normal on-road use, Y62 front axle shafts can last 100,000 km or more. In UAE conditions with regular off-road use, high heat, and sand exposure, boots can crack within 60,000 to 80,000 km, particularly if they have never been inspected. We recommend checking the CV boots at every major service, which on the Y62 means at least once a year given UAE conditions.

Is it safe to drive a Nissan Patrol Y62 with a torn CV boot in Dubai?

A torn CV boot with no joint noise is drivable for a short period, but the joint is now exposed to sand and moisture and will deteriorate quickly. In desert conditions like the runs to Al Qudra or Big Red, contamination accelerates and the joint can fail within days. A fully failed joint can cause loss of drive and in severe cases affect steering control at speed. Book the repair promptly.
