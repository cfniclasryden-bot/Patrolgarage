Y61 Snorkel Fitting Cost

UPDATED · MAY 2026

Quick answer: Fitting a snorkel to a Y61 Super Safari in Dubai typically costs between AED 800 and AED 2,200 all-in, depending on the brand of snorkel kit and whether any bodywork modification is needed. That price covers the snorkel body, intake ram, fitting hardware, and labour at a specialist Patrol workshop in areas like Ras Al Khor or Al Quoz. Budget an extra AED 200 to AED 400 if your A-pillar panel needs repainting after the cut.

The Y61 Super Safari has been sold in the GCC continuously since 1997, and in the UAE it is still the go-to choice for serious off-road work. Head out to Big Red in Al Ain, the Liwa dunes, or the softer tracks around Al Qudra and you will notice that most of the working Patrols have a snorkel fitted. There is a good reason for that. Dubai summers push ambient temperatures to 45°C to 50°C, and the sand and dust that travel with that heat are hard on any air filtration system. A low-mounted factory airbox pulls hot, dusty air from directly above the engine bay. A snorkel relocates the intake point to roof height, where the air is cooler and cleaner, and it gives you genuine water-crossing ability without risking a hydrolocked engine. For the Y61 specifically, which uses a mechanically straightforward TB48DE 4.8-litre inline-six or the older TB45E 4.5-litre depending on year, protecting the intake is one of the smartest and cheapest modifications you can make before hitting the desert. This guide covers exactly what a snorkel fitting costs here in Dubai in 2026, what the job involves, and what to watch out for.

What is a snorkel and why does a Y61 Super Safari need one in the UAE?

A snorkel is a raised air intake pipe that runs from the engine airbox up along the A-pillar to roof height, giving the engine access to cleaner, cooler air and protecting it during water crossings.

The Y61's factory airbox sits low in the engine bay, which is fine for road use but becomes a liability in the UAE desert. Fine silica sand is abrasive in ways that coarser gravel is not. When you are running through a bowl at Liwa, rooster-tailing sand behind another vehicle, or crossing a sabkha flat where the surface breaks into a dust cloud, a low intake ingests grit that accelerates filter wear and, over time, gets past the filter into the cylinders. With a snorkel, the inlet sits in cleaner air above the dust layer. Per the Environment Agency Abu Dhabi, the UAE's desert terrain regularly records particulate matter levels that exceed urban averages, which is relevant to any vehicle using a low-mounted air intake in off-road conditions.

Water crossings matter too. The UAE has more flood events than people expect, particularly in Ras Al Khaimah and the mountain routes near Hatta. The Y61 with a standard airbox has a wading depth of roughly 500mm. A properly fitted snorkel extends that to around 800mm depending on other sealing. That is a meaningful difference when you are picking a line across a wadi.

What does a Y61 Super Safari snorkel kit include?

A complete kit for the Y61 includes the snorkel body, a ram-head intake at the top, all mounting hardware, a replacement air hose, and the template for cutting the A-pillar.

The main brands sold and fitted in the UAE are Safari Snorkels (Australian), ARB, and a number of aftermarket brands from China and Thailand that are priced lower but vary in quality. Safari Snorkels and ARB kits are rotationally moulded polyethylene, which handles the UV radiation and heat of a UAE summer without cracking. Some of the cheaper kits use injection-moulded ABS that can become brittle within a couple of seasons. We have seen them split along the seam where the mounting bracket sits, which lets dust straight in. The polyethylene kits are worth the extra cost here.

For the Y61, Safari Snorkels' part reference is SS780HF (TB48DE engines) [NEEDS_SOURCE for exact current part number verification from Safari]. ARB makes a direct equivalent. Both require cutting the A-pillar and inner guard to route the intake hose, so the fitting is not reversible without bodywork.

What does snorkel fitting cost in Dubai in 2026?

Total cost at a Dubai specialist workshop for a Y61 Super Safari snorkel fit runs from AED 800 at the low end (budget kit, no paint) to AED 2,200 for a premium brand kit with professional panel prep.

Here is how the cost breaks down in practice:

  • Budget aftermarket snorkel kit (import brand): AED 250 to AED 450
  • Safari Snorkels or ARB kit for Y61: AED 600 to AED 950
  • Labour for fitting, cutting, and sealing: AED 300 to AED 500
  • A-pillar touch-up paint or respray: AED 200 to AED 400
  • Replacement air hose or filter upgrade (optional): AED 100 to AED 200

If you source the kit yourself and bring it in, most Ras Al Khor and Al Quoz workshops will charge AED 300 to AED 450 labour-only. If you want the workshop to supply the kit as well, the total is usually bundled and the numbers above apply. Workshops in Deira and Sharjah Industrial Area often price slightly lower on labour, though lead times vary.

One cost people overlook: the A-pillar cut is permanent. If the panel is not masked and prepped properly before cutting, overspray or bare metal gets exposed. Factor in that paint cost from the start rather than coming back to fix it later.

How long does fitting take and what does the job involve?

A straightforward Y61 snorkel fit takes two to three hours in the workshop, assuming the kit is on hand and the body panel is in reasonable condition.

The process goes like this. The workshop removes the factory air intake hose and airbox lid, then templates the A-pillar using the guide in the kit. The hole is cut with a jigsaw or stepped drill depending on the shape. The inner guard may need a secondary cut for the hose routing. The snorkel body bolts to the A-pillar using the supplied hardware, and the hose connects down to the airbox. The ram head is oriented to face forward (for maximum pressure-ram effect at speed) or rearward (for better dust exclusion when stationary in a dusty environment). Forward-facing is the more common choice here for dune driving where speed varies.

Sealing around the panel cut is done with body sealant or self-amalgamating tape. This step matters. A poorly sealed cut lets water into the A-pillar cavity, which leads to rust over time. The Y61 A-pillar is steel, not aluminium, so moisture is a real concern even in a dry climate when there is coast humidity involved.

Does a snorkel affect your vehicle registration or RTA inspection in Dubai?

A snorkel is a body modification and technically falls under the category of vehicle alterations in the UAE. Per the RTA, any modification that changes the exterior profile of a vehicle is subject to approval, though in practice snorkels on 4x4s are widely inspected without issue at Tasjeel and Wasel centres when the fitment is clean and does not obstruct the driver's field of vision or affect safety systems.

The A-pillar is within the driver's peripheral sightline. A properly fitted snorkel body that sits flush and does not protrude excessively into the mirror zone has not been a problem for Y61 owners at annual inspection. Loose, badly fitted kits that rattle or show signs of structural compromise are a different matter. Fit it properly and this is not a concern in practice.

If you are planning to take your Y61 across the border into Oman for desert trips, Oman's regulations are broadly similar and snorkels are common on vehicles at the border. No special paperwork is typically required for a snorkel specifically.

What else should you check when fitting a snorkel to a Y61?

A snorkel fitting is a good opportunity to service the air filter, inspect the airbox for cracks or debris, and check the intake hose for any age-related cracking, particularly on high-mileage Y61s that have been in the UAE heat for several years.

The TB48DE engine, used in Y61s from around 2004 onward, has a large-volume airbox that pairs well with a high-flow filter upgrade (K&N or equivalent) when fitting a snorkel. You will not see major power gains, but you will get slightly better filtration efficiency and easier breathing at the intake. Cost for a high-flow filter for the Y61 runs around AED 150 to AED 280 depending on brand.

While the workshop has access to the A-pillar and front corner, it is also worth asking them to inspect the front chassis rails for any corrosion or sand packing, particularly if the vehicle has done significant dune work. The Y61 chassis is generally solid but the front jacking points and outriggers collect sand that holds moisture against the metal. Catching this early is straightforward. Ignoring it is expensive later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a snorkel fitting cost for a Y61 Super Safari in Dubai in 2026?

A complete snorkel fitting including kit and labour costs between AED 800 and AED 2,200 at a Patrol specialist in Dubai. The lower end uses budget aftermarket kits. The higher end covers branded kits like Safari Snorkels or ARB with proper panel prep and touch-up paint. Labour alone, if you supply the kit, is typically AED 300 to AED 450.

Which snorkel brand fits a Y61 Super Safari and which is best for UAE conditions?

Safari Snorkels and ARB both make direct-fit kits for the Y61 Super Safari. Both use rotationally moulded polyethylene that handles UAE UV and heat better than cheaper ABS alternatives. For desert and dune use in Dubai, either brand is a reliable choice. Budget import kits are available for less but can crack within a few seasons in the UAE climate.

Will a snorkel pass the RTA inspection in Dubai?

In practice, properly fitted snorkels on Y61 Super Safaris pass Tasjeel and Wasel annual inspections without issue. The snorkel must not obstruct the driver's field of vision and must be securely fitted with no loose components. Per the RTA, exterior body modifications are subject to approval, but cleanly fitted 4x4 snorkels are standard and widely accepted at inspection.

How long does snorkel fitting take at a Dubai workshop?

Fitting takes two to three hours at a workshop that has done Y61 snorkel jobs before. First-time fits on a particular model can take longer if the template or hose routing needs adjustment. Book it as a half-day job and the vehicle is ready same day in almost every case.
